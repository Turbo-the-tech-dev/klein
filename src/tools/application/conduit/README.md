# Conduit Application

*heavy breathing* 🫁 Conduit - Protect the conductors. Protect the people.

## Overview

Conduit applications involve the installation of raceway systems to protect and route electrical conductors. Proper conduit work requires skill and the right tools.

## NEC Requirements

*heavy breathing* Conduit must comply with:
- NEC Article 342 - Intermediate Metal Conduit
- NEC Article 358 - Electrical Metallic Tubing
- NEC Article 344 - Rigid Metal Conduit
- NEC Article 352 - PVC Conduit
- Proper support spacing
- Bend radius requirements
