## NEC Requirements

*heavy breathing* Drilling must comply with:
- NEC Article 300.4 - Protection against physical damage
- NEC Article 300.5 - Underground installations
- Proper hole sizing for conductors
- Boring through framing members

## Safety Requirements

⚠️ **WEAR** eye protection always
⚠️ **SECURE** workpiece before drilling
⚠️ **CHECK** for hidden utilities
⚠️ **USE** proper bit for material
⚠️ **LET** the tool do the work - don't force
